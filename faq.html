FAQ

Questions readers ask.

Which book should I read first?

If you want a story, start with Palm Kernel and The gods or Miss Broke Lynn. If you prefer non-fiction, To Create Is Divine is a short, sharp read you can finish in a sitting.

What do you write about?

Money, tradition, creativity and everyday African life. My books range from Igbo folklore to contemporary comedy to satire and non-fiction.

Are your books connected?

No, each one stands alone. They share a world and a set of concerns more than they share characters.

Where do your ideas come from?

From African life as I see it: the village and the city, the old gods and the new hustles, and the money questions sitting underneath all of it.
